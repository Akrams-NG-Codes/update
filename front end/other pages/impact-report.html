Impact report

We measure impact in relationships, confidence, and opportunity.

Acromind Initiative focuses on creating lasting value for young people, families, and communities through safe, creative participation.

At a glance

What we aim to build.

ConfidenceHelping children and youth feel capable, brave, and accepted.
CreativityOpening space for imagination, expression, and playful learning.
BelongingCreating welcoming communities where young people are seen and heard.
LeadershipSupporting initiative, teamwork, and practical life skills.

How we understand progress

We view progress as more than attendance or performance. We look for meaningful changes in confidence, participation, friendships, and the courage to try again.

Our programming helps young people build creative confidence, explore new identities, and become more connected to trusted adults and peers. That sense of safety and support can transform how a young person sees their future.

What we continue to improve

  • Clearer safeguarding and welfare practices
  • Stronger local partnerships
  • More transparent communication with supporters
  • Ongoing opportunities for youth leadership and mentorship
