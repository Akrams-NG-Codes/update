Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you get in touch.

What does Acromind Initiative do?

We use circus arts and creative expression to help children and young people build confidence, skills, and supportive community connections.

Where does Acromind work?

We are based in Uganda and work with partner communities. Contact us to ask about current activities and opportunities in your area.

How can I take part or volunteer?

We welcome enquiries about volunteering, sharing practical skills, and supporting workshops or community events. Send us a message with your interests and availability.

How can I support the work?

You can ask about workshop support, youth opportunities, or a partnership. We will share current giving instructions when you contact us.

Can I donate directly through this website?

No. The website does not process payments. Please contact us to confirm current giving methods and details before sending funds.

How do I raise a safeguarding concern?

Contact us directly at olangoacrobat@gmail.com and let us know the nature of your concern.

When should I expect a reply?

We aim to reply within a few working days.
