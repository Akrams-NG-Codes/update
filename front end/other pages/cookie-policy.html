Privacy and transparency

Cookie policy.

How this website uses browser storage and third-party checkout services.

Last updated: 6 October 2026

What this site stores

Acromind Initiative does not use advertising cookies. The site uses browser local storage to remember your cookie choice. If you allow optional storage, it keeps up to 50 recent site interaction events in your browser to help assess this website. Those records are not sent to Acromind Initiative or another analytics provider.

Your choices

You can choose essential storage only or allow optional storage using the notice shown on your first visit. You can change your choice at any time with the “Cookie settings” control in the footer. Choosing essential storage only removes the optional interaction record from this browser. Your choice is stored in this browser so the notice does not appear on every page.

Pesapal checkout

The donation page links to Pesapal, an independent payment provider. The embedded checkout is not loaded until you allow optional services or choose to load it yourself. When you use Pesapal, it may store cookies or similar technologies under its own privacy practices. Please review Pesapal’s privacy policy for details.

Managing browser storage

You can also clear local storage and cookies through your browser settings. Clearing the saved choice will cause the cookie notice to appear again.
