Creative expression

Where confidence takes centre stage

Confidence can grow one small attempt at a time. In a circus workshop, a young person can practise a new movement, try again, and share progress with peers. The activity matters, but so does the encouragement around it.

Every chance to participate can help a young person feel seen, develop persistence, and imagine what they might do next.

Education and opportunity

A new chapter for Josephine.

Josephine is a talented, passionate young performer whose journey continues to inspire us. Last year, she was deeply involved in AcroMind and stood out as a performer on stage.

At one point, she had lost hope of returning to school because her mother could not afford the fees. Her dream felt out of reach.

Today, Josephine is back in school, thanks to a full bursary from JANAN Schools. This opportunity gives her a chance to continue her education and keep pursuing her dream.

Josephine's story is not hers alone. Many girls are full of talent and potential but lack opportunity. They deserve a chance, too.

We are so proud of you, Josephine. Keep going; your dream is coming true.

We also thank Clowner utan Gränser for project support that has helped us connect, grow, and find support for young talents like Josephine.
