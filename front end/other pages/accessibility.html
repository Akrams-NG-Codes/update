Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to access our work.

We are working to make the information and experience on this website easier to use for all visitors.

Our approach

Acromind Initiative aims to make its website readable, navigable, and usable on a variety of devices and screen sizes. We use clear headings, alt text, good contrast, and straightforward page layouts.

What we are improving

  • Readability with good contrast and clear typography
  • Keyboard-friendly navigation
  • Meaningful link labels and descriptive headings
  • Image alt text and accessible page structure

Need help?

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of the site, please contact us at olangoacrobat@gmail.com and let us know what you need. We are happy to support accessible communication.
