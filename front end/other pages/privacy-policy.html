Privacy policy

Your information matters.

Acromind Initiative is committed to protecting the privacy and dignity of the people we work with.

1. Information we collect

We may collect basic information when you contact us, sign up for opportunities, or support our work. This may include your name, email address, phone number, organisation, and the details of your enquiry.

2. How we use your information

We use information to respond to enquiries, coordinate volunteer or partnership opportunities, manage donations, and keep in touch about relevant activities and updates.

3. How we protect information

We take reasonable steps to keep personal information secure and only share it with trusted team members or partners when necessary to support the work of Acromind Initiative.

4. Third-party services

We may use email and website tools to manage communications and donations. These services are used only to support the organisation’s work and are reviewed for appropriate privacy practices.

5. Your choices

You can ask to update, correct, or stop receiving non-essential communications at any time by contacting us at olangoacrobat@gmail.com.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please get in touch with us using the contact page or email us directly.
