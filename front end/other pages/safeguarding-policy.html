Safeguarding

Children and young people are at the heart of our work.

We create safe, respectful environments where every person can participate with dignity and confidence.

Our safeguarding commitment

Acromind Initiative is committed to ensuring that all children, young people, and adults involved in our programmes are protected from harm, abuse, and neglect. We recognise that safeguarding is everyone’s responsibility.

What this means in practice

  • We create spaces that are physically and emotionally safe.
  • We promote respectful communication and positive relationships.
  • We support role clarity, supervision, and accountability.
  • We respond to concerns seriously and confidentially.

Reporting a concern

If you are worried about safeguarding, child protection, or harmful behaviour linked to Acromind Initiative, please contact us immediately. We will listen carefully, document the concern, and respond in a way that prioritises safety and support.

Contact: olangoacrobat@gmail.com with the subject line “Safeguarding concern”.

Working with dignity

We believe that every person deserves to participate in a safe environment free from discrimination, exploitation, and harm. This principle guides how we plan activities, support participants, and work with partners.
