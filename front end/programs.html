Our programmes

Creative tools for a stronger start in life.

Acromind Initiative uses circus arts, play, and mentorship to create opportunities for children and youth in Uganda.

What we do

Skills, confidence, and belonging grow through practice.

Our programmes bring young people together in supportive spaces where movement and creative expression make learning active.

01 / CREATIVE EXPRESSION

Circus for development

Acrobatics, juggling, and performance invite participants to practise coordination, courage, persistence, and self-expression.

02 / YOUTH LEADERSHIP

Skills for life

Group projects help young people practise communication, collaboration, decision-making, and leadership.

03 / SAFE SPACES

Community connections

Mentors, peers, and families help create a dependable network where young people can participate and belong.

In practice

Learning through movement and teamwork.
