About Acromind Initiative

Young people are at the centre of everything we do.

We use circus arts and creative expression to help children and youth in Uganda build confidence, connection, and a sense of possibility.

Who we are

A community built around courage and belonging.

AcroMind Initiative is a Uganda-based non-profit transforming the lives of vulnerable children and youth through circus arts. Every workshop ends with a small showcase, and every round of applause becomes a reminder that this young person has worth.

Our story

We began with a belief that circus arts could help young people see new possibilities for their lives.

Our impact approach

What our programmes are designed to support.

We aim to create opportunities for children and young people to build confidence, creativity, belonging, and leadership through participation.

ConfidenceEncouraging young people to practise, persist, and express themselves.
CreativityMaking room for imagination, play, and creative learning.
BelongingSupporting positive relationships with peers, mentors, and community.
LeadershipHelping young people build collaboration and decision-making skills.

These are intended outcomes, not published participation or outcome figures. Read our impact approach.
